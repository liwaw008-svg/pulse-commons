## Settlement

The first pulse receives 45, the second 35, and the third receives the final 20. Nothing is hidden and nothing is ranked by reputation. The order submitted to the epoch is the order used by the contract.

Pulse Commons separates one semantic question from one deterministic allocation. GenLayer validators authenticate the public metric describing the resource. Once that metric is frozen, closing the epoch uses the smallest possible rule: give each pulse the lesser of its request and the remaining capacity.

The contract rejects mismatched labels and values, repeat settlement, and settlement by anyone other than the host. It preserves truncated allocations so a participant can distinguish scarcity from arbitrary preference.

`epoch-anchor.json` is this commons’ chain anchor. It names the epoch contract, opening transaction, host, network, and allocation rule in the language of the project rather than in a shared template.
